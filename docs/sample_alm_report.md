## Interpretation

- Base surplus is negative at -1,159,005.
- In unscaled terms, liabilities have longer modified duration than assets.
- The funded liability duration exceeds asset duration, so first-order surplus sensitivity tends to benefit from higher rates and suffer from lower rates.
- The curve is upward-sloping between 2Y and 10Y.
- The most adverse deterministic stress is `parallel_down_100bps` with surplus change -305,400.
- The most favorable deterministic stress is `parallel_up_100bps` with surplus change 263,296.
- Largest absolute surplus PV01 is around 10Y.
- The largest annual cash-flow mismatch occurs in the 1-2Y bucket.
- The final cumulative net cash-flow gap is -2,146,387.
- For a +100 bps parallel shock, duration approximation error is visible relative to full revaluation.

## Limitations

- Fixed-rate bullet bonds are simplified and accrued-free.
- Day-count conventions, settlement calendars and accrued interest are not modelled yet.
- There is no credit spread curve, default model or liquidity premium layer yet.
- Liability inputs are cash-flow schedules, not actuarial projection models.
- ECB curve ingestion is public-data ingestion, not a production market-data stack.
- Rate stresses are deterministic; there is no PCA or stochastic curve model yet.
